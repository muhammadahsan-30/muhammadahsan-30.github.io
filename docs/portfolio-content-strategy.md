# Homepage content strategy

A first-principles reconsideration of the homepage as **one story**, not a set of sections
to improve individually. The visual system from `portfolio-design-spec.md` is preserved
unchanged; this document is about what is said, in what order, and what is cut.

---

## 1. Diagnosis of the current homepage

Current order: hero → *One thread* → *Flagship* → *Selected work* → *Published* →
*How I work* → closing.

Four problems, found by reading the page rather than defending it:

**The Smog article is told three times.** Once as a card title, once as a "Read in Daily
Pakistan" button, once as a standalone *Published* section repeating the same 1,150+ /
207 / 292,800 figures. One piece of published writing, three tellings.

**"One thread" is a meta-section.** It is a section *about* the structure of the
portfolio. It tells the reader a pattern exists instead of arranging the page so the
pattern is unavoidable. It also front-loads the Kinetic story before the reader has any
reason to care about Kinetic.

**"Flagship" and "Selected work" are category labels doing work the content should do.**
Worse, they are siblings: calling one project *Flagship* and the rest *Selected work*
demotes the other two by category rather than by scale. Smog is published in a national
newspaper. That is not a lesser category of thing.

**Experience disappeared.** Removing the status rows took KPMG off the homepage except as
one clause in the subline and as repeated link text inside the skills list. The audit
found the finance lens was the best-served on the site; on the current homepage it is now
the worst-served.

Plus one redundancy: **"How I work"** restates in list form what the projects already
prove in evidence form.

---

## 2. The pattern, and where it belongs

The repeated shape across all four pieces of work:

| | Context | Gap | What I made |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kinetic | Reconciled CAD 1m+ of media spend inside the agency | It only ever ran after the campaign ended | The OOH analyzer |
| KPMG | Valuation work down to the journal entries | — | DCF and three-statement models |
| Smog | Lahore's hoardings and its air | Nobody had costed the retrofit | A feasibility model, published |
| BarakahLink | Giving platforms assume a smartphone | Many who need them have neither | SMS-accessible matching |

**The conclusion: the pattern is not a section, it is the shape of every entry.** If each
piece of work is presented as *context → gap → what I made → proof*, the reader induces
the pattern by the third entry without ever being told it exists. A section announcing it
is weaker than three entries demonstrating it.

So the "Saw / Asked / Built / Learned" device from the brief becomes the **structure of
each project's expanded panel**, and a compressed version of it becomes each project's
**one-sentence summary**. Implicit by default, explicit on demand.

## 3. Three homepage narrative options

### Option A — Credibility first

```
  Identity + positioning
  Where I've worked          KPMG · Kinetic · Waterloo, compact
  The work                   OOH (large) · Smog · BarakahLink
  Capabilities
  Contact
```

**For:** leads with brand names, which is how consulting and finance recruiters screen.
KPMG inside the first scroll.
**Against:** the first screenful is a résumé. The thing that actually differentiates him —
that he builds — arrives third. A reader who bounces early leaves with "Waterloo student
who interned at KPMG", which is a category, not a person.

### Option B — Work first

```
  Identity + positioning
  OOH                        full treatment
  Smog
  BarakahLink
  Experience
  Capabilities
  Contact
```

**For:** leads with the strongest artifact. Highest ceiling for a reader who scrolls.
**Against:** the origin is what makes the OOH project impressive — a second-year student
building a media-analytics product is interesting *because* he was inside the agency. Put
the experience after, and the first entry reads as a competent student project rather than
a response to something he lived. The best material is spent before it is set up.

### Option C — Woven *(recommended)*

```
  Opening unit               identity + positioning + proof, as ONE block
  01  OOH Analyzer           origin line → artifact → evidence → case study
  02  Smog                   origin line → the published article as the artifact
  03  BarakahLink            origin line → product artifact
  Where I've worked          compact, three roles, → Experience
  Contact
```

**For:** every entry carries its own origin, so the pattern is felt three times rather
than asserted once. Credibility arrives in the opening unit (Waterloo, KPMG, Kinetic, the
two real figures) without a separate résumé section. No meta-section, no category labels,
no duplication.
**Against:** Kinetic is mentioned twice — once as the OOH origin, once in the experience
strip. That is acceptable: the first is narrative, the second is factual, and they serve
different readers.

### Recommendation: **Option C**

It is the only one of the three that makes the brief's pattern structural rather than
stated, and it is the only one that removes all four diagnosed problems at once. It also
answers §5 of the brief directly — identity, credibility and evidence land in a single
opening unit instead of across three sections.

## 4. Revised section order

```
  ─ opening unit ──────────────────────────────────────────────
    eyebrow · name · positioning line · subline
    two real figures + two credentials, in one row
    actions

  ─ 01  OOH Campaign Performance Analyzer ─────────────────────
    one-sentence summary carrying context → gap → built
    large theme-aware artifact
    three project figures, labelled synthetic
    View details ↓   ·   Explore the case study →

  ─ 02  Smog-Eating Billboard ─────────────────────────────────
    one-sentence summary
    the published article, presented AS the artifact
    View details ↓   ·   Read in Daily Pakistan ↗

  ─ 03  BarakahLink ───────────────────────────────────────────
    one-sentence summary
    product artifact
    View details ↓   ·   Open live ↗

  ─ Where I've worked ─────────────────────────────────────────
    KPMG · Kinetic · Waterloo — three lines, → Experience

  ─ Closing ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

No "Flagship". No "Selected work". No "Published" section. No "How I work". Projects are
numbered 01–03, which gives editorial order without ranking language — and OOH is
obviously the largest because it has the largest artifact, the most evidence and a case
study behind it.

## 5. Revised hero

The brief's §5 asks for identity + credibility + evidence + curiosity without scrolling
through multiple introductory sections. That means folding the stat band into the opening
rather than treating it as a separate band.

```
  WATERLOO, ONTARIO   (AVAILABLE JAN–APR 2027)

  Muhammad
  Ahsan Sheikh

  I learn how the work actually gets done —
  then build what's missing.

  Honours Mathematics and Business Administration at Waterloo.
  Valuation models at KPMG, media campaigns at a WPP/GroupM agency,
  and the tools that measure them.

  ┌─────────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────────┬──────────────┐
  │ KPMG            │ Kinetic         │ CAD 19.5M       │ CAD 1M+      │
  │ Deal Advisory   │ WPP/GroupM, 1   │ capital in a    │ media spend  │
  │ 2026            │ of 2 interns    │ venture valued  │ reconciled   │
  └─────────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┴──────────────┘
  From paid work. Project figures appear with the project.

  [Resume] [Email] [LinkedIn] [GitHub]
```

**The change:** the band now carries **two employers and two figures** rather than three
bare numbers. A number without an employer attached is a trivia question; "CAD 19.5M"
means something once "KPMG, Deal Advisory" sits beside it. This is also what restores
KPMG to the homepage without a separate résumé section.

"1 of 2 interns" moves from its own tile into the Kinetic tile, where it belongs as
context rather than competing as a headline statistic.

### Positioning line

**Recommended:** *"I learn how the work actually gets done — then build what's missing."*

Changed from *"I learn how a business works"*. "How the work actually gets done" is more
specific and more accurate to the evidence: the journal entries at KPMG, the five-department
rotation at Kinetic, the billboard cleaning cycle, SMS access. "How a business works" is
the kind of thing a strategy consultant says; this is operational, which is what he
actually did.

**Alternates considered and rejected:**
- *"I go to where the numbers are made."* — evocative but says nothing about building
- *"Business understanding, analytical depth, technical execution."* — a list of nouns
- The current line — good, slightly more abstract than the evidence supports

## 6. Revised project summaries

Each leads with the problem, not the stack. Each is one sentence containing context → gap
→ what was built.

**01 · OOH Campaign Performance Analyzer**
> Reconciling media spend inside a WPP/GroupM agency only ever found under-delivering
> sites after the campaign had ended. This finds them while it is still running — and
> prices what the shortfall is worth.

Visible beneath: 10.5m impressions short under a campaign number reading −2.1% · 4 days to
flag against 31 to reconciliation · 44 unit tests. Then *Synthetic dataset · Python · SQL ·
SQLite*.

**02 · Smog-Eating Billboard**
> Lahore has 1,150+ advertising hoardings sitting in some of the worst air on earth. I
> modelled whether coating them with photocatalytic titanium dioxide would pay for itself
> — and found the argument that works is commercial, not environmental.

**03 · BarakahLink**
> Giving platforms assume a smartphone and a stable connection, and the people who most
> need them have neither. Built around SMS access and hyper-local matching instead.

### Expanded panel structure — the recurring device

```
  Saw        what was actually happening, from the inside
  Asked      what was missing, and why it mattered
  Built      the concrete work
  Learned    what came out of it — the memorable part
  Depth      stack chips, figures, links
```

Four beats, same order every time. By the third project a reader recognises the shape,
which is how the pattern gets communicated without a section announcing it.

## 7. Revised experience treatment

**On the homepage:** a compact three-row strip near the end — company, role, one line,
dates — linking to the Experience page. It confirms rather than introduces, because the
opening unit already carries the brand names and each project carries its origin.

**On the Experience page:** unchanged from the current build. Disclosure per role,
Justdiggit included, the Kinetic → OOH "led to" link. That page is working.

**Why not experience earlier (Option A):** because each project now carries its own origin
inline. A reader learns *Kinetic → reconciliation → analyzer* inside entry 01, which is
more persuasive than reading it in an experience list and having to connect it themselves.

---

## 8. Skills / capabilities decision

**Cut from the homepage entirely.**

The projects already prove Python, SQL, SQLite, testing, financial modelling, market
research and LLM integration — in evidence form, which is stronger than list form. The
Experience page carries the grouped toolkit for anyone who wants the inventory.

**The trade-off, stated honestly:** a recruiter who skims only the homepage and never
expands a project will not see the word "Python" in a list. It is mitigated because each
project's visible (unexpanded) line carries its stack — *Synthetic dataset · Python · SQL ·
SQLite* sits under entry 01 without a click — and because the Experience page exists. If
keyword scanning later proves to matter more than editorial economy, the toolkit block can
be added back in one commit.

## 9. Content to remove

| Removed | Reason |
|---|---|
| "One thread" section | Meta-section. The pattern is now structural across the three entries. |
| "Flagship" / "Selected work" labels | Category labels doing work the content should do |
| Standalone "Published" section | Third telling of the Smog article on one page |
| "How I work" skills section | Restates in list form what the projects prove in evidence form |
| "1 of 2 interns" as a standalone statistic | Folded into the Kinetic tile as context |
| Duplicate "All projects →" and closing CTA rows | One action row per block |
| Generic section intros | Every remaining line establishes credibility, explains a problem, shows evidence, or invites exploration |

## 10. Visual opportunities using real artifacts

| Entry | Artifact | Status |
|---|---|---|
| OOH | The app itself — overview with the waterfall, Attention Centre, flight timeline, market heatmap. Theme-aware light/dark pairs. | ✅ captured |
| Smog | **The published article presented as the artifact**, using the existing `.press` component as the card's visual, plus the billboard mockup | mockup ✅, press treatment to build |
| BarakahLink | A real screenshot of the live product | ⚠️ currently a hot-linked `thum.io` render with an `onerror` fallback — **capture and commit a static image** |

The Smog change is the most interesting: its artifact is not a mockup, it is *a national
newspaper running the argument*. Presenting the publication itself as the card's visual
material is both more honest and more impressive than a concept render.

---

## 11. Where the wow factor comes from

1. **The opening unit.** Identity, positioning, two employers and two real figures in one
   block, before any scrolling. A recruiter has the whole case in five seconds.
2. **Three entries at genuinely different scales**, each with its own real artifact — an
   application, a newspaper, a product — rather than three cards of equal weight.
3. **The recurring Saw / Asked / Built / Learned shape**, discovered rather than announced.
4. **Theme-aware screenshots**: the analyzer has its own light and dark modes, so the
   preview follows the reader's theme instead of sitting as a bright rectangle.
5. **What was taken away.** A page with no meta-section, no duplicate article, no skills
   list and no category labels reads as edited — and edited reads as confident.

---

## 12. Unchanged

Newsreader / Inter, the cream–sienna–teal palette, the dot grid, dark mode, the `.rv`
reveal, the disclosure component and its 260ms `grid-template-rows` animation, the card and
`.shot` treatments, section numbering, the editorial tone. No new colour, typeface, shadow
or motion type.

`ooh.html`, `experience.html`, `projects.html` and `contact.html` keep their current
structure. This pass is the homepage, plus the Smog press treatment and the BarakahLink
artifact, both of which also apply to `projects.html`.
