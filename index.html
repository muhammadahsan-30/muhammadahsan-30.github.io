Smog-Eating BillboardPublished

Oct 2024

Daily Pakistan · October 2024

Lahore has 1,150+ advertising hoardings and some of the worst air on earth. I modelled whether coating them would pay for itself.

The case for advertisers is commercial before environmental — smog already forces a costlier cleaning cycle than the retrofit would.

The problem

Lahore's air quality index hit 207 in January 2024. The city also has over a thousand large advertising surfaces sitting in direct sun — the exact condition photocatalytic coatings need.

What I built

  • A model estimating pollutant removal from retrofitting 1,150+ hoardings — equivalent to 292,800 trees over six hours
  • A supply-side check that found the binding constraint: titanium dioxide is not produced in Pakistan
  • A cost-benefit case written for the party who would actually pay for it

Why it matters

The environmental argument does not move a media owner. The cleaning-cycle argument does — smog discolours conventional billboard skins faster, so the replacement cycle already costs more than the coating would. Reframing the case on cost is what made it publishable.

BarakahLinkLive

Jan 2026

Giving platforms assume a smartphone and a stable connection. Many who need them have neither.

The problem

Donation-redistribution works locally or not at all, and the people who most need it are the least likely to have an app and reliable data.

What I built

  • Unstructured input handling. Google Gemini turns messy free-text offers into organised listings and need summaries, written to preserve the dignity of the person asking
  • SMS-style access through a simulated gateway, so the platform works without an app
  • Hyper-local matching so donations surface to people close enough to actually collect them

Why it matters

The constraint that mattered was connectivity, not features. Designing for SMS first changed what the product could be rather than how it looked.
