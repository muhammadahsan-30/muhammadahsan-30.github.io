02

Smog-Eating Billboard

Feasibility researchPublished

Oct 2024

Lahore has 1,150+ advertising hoardings in direct sun and an air quality index of 207 (January 2024). I modeled whether coating that surface with photocatalytic titanium dioxide would move air quality enough to justify the cost.

  • Modeled the effect of retrofitting Lahore's 1,150+ hoardings with photocatalytic coatings, estimating pollutant removal equivalent to 292,800 trees over six hours
  • Identified the supply constraint: titanium dioxide is not produced in Pakistan
  • Built the case on cost-benefit grounds, which is the version that persuades a media owner: smog discolours conventional billboard skins faster, so the cleaning and replacement cycle already costs advertisers more than the retrofit would

A donation-redistribution platform built for communities where the constraint is connectivity. Most giving platforms assume a smartphone and stable data; many who need them have neither.

  • Unstructured input handling. Google Gemini turns messy, free-text donation offers into organized listings and need summaries written to preserve the dignity of the person asking
  • SMS-style access through a simulated gateway, so the platform works without an app or reliable data
  • Hyper-local mapping so donations surface to people close enough to actually collect them, rather than to whoever happens to be browsing
